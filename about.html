About Datalink

A Ghanaian developer building AI agents for Ghanaian businesses.

Why I Built Datalink

I watched Ghanaian business owners lose sales every night — not because their products were bad, but because they couldn't answer WhatsApp messages at 11pm. By morning, the customer had moved on. The sale was gone.

The technology to fix this existed. But every tool I found was built for American or European businesses — priced in dollars, designed for different markets, with no understanding of how business actually works in Ghana. Nobody was responding in Twi. Nobody understood Mobile Money. Nobody cared about Jiji sellers or Accra boutiques.

Datalink is my answer to that. I build WhatsApp AI agents specifically for Ghanaian businesses — trained on your exact products, connected to your existing WhatsApp number, priced at what makes sense here. When something breaks, you reach me directly, in Ghana, the same day.
